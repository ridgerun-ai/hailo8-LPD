RidgeRun License Plate Detection

What is RR LPD?

RR LPD is an edge-ready library for real-time license plate detection, powered by the Hailo-8 accelerator. It runs fully on-premise, processes live streams or MP4s with low latency, and returns annotated video.

🚀 If you are interested on this or any other application for your project visit our portfolio.

Demo Instructions

  1. Upload a video using the component on the left side or select the example video.
  2. Press the Process button located in the left side.
  3. Wait while it processes and View the result in the right side component.
